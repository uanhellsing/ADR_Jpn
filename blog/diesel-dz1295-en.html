Diesel DZ1295

Article language

In May 2015 I bought a Diesel DZ1295 — a different kind of item from the PCs and audio gear, but one worth keeping in the same timeline.

It is a large Diesel-style quartz watch with a stainless-steel case, black dial and black leather strap. The model includes a calendar, mineral crystal and 100 m water resistance.

The photo dated 27 May 2015 captures it almost immediately after purchase, still sitting in its box.
