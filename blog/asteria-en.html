Asteria: bringing an Ultimate Works RVS back to life

I could not walk past a 13,000-yen LED lightsaber sitting in the junk section at BOOKOFF. I own a soldering iron and have some basic electronics skills, so it looked like exactly the kind of adventure worth trying.

After spending time on diagnostics, soldering and Asteria configuration, the dead saber turned into a fully working example.

Manufacturer: Ultimate Works · hilt: RVS (Darth Revan) · soundboard: Asteria V2.5
