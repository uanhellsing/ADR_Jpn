John Coltrane & Johnny Hartman

A new addition to the collection: John Coltrane and Johnny Hartman. I finally picked up the record after it had been recommended to me on Amazon.

The original album was released by Impulse! Records in 1963. Coltrane’s restrained tenor and Hartman’s deep baritone fit together so naturally that this is the kind of record I want to play from beginning to end.

Album: 1963 · Label: Impulse! · Format: LP
